## Geometry gate

The programme will use the word genuine geometry only after specifying objects/configurations, transport, composition, a local connection/defect object, loop holonomy, covariance/gauge structure, and an operational or representation-independent evaluation procedure.

## Literature boundary required before publication

The final paper must explicitly distinguish the programme from established work on:

- multiplicative domains of CP/UCP maps;
- decoherence-free/noiseless algebras and subsystems;
- Uhlmann/Jamiolkowski channel holonomy and geometric phases;
- noncommutative carré du champ / quantum Markov semigroups;
- Lindbladian learning and process tomography;
- classical shadows/randomized measurements;
- process tensors and quantum combs.

No priority claim such as first/new physical curvature is permitted without a dedicated literature audit.
