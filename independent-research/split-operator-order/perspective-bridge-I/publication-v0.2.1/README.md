## Scientific role

This is a Perspective + Research Programme bridging:

1. Article I — exact contextual flatness and pair-reduced loop defects, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.22289201`;
2. Article II — UCP multiplicativity defects, GKSL first-order loop defects, and sharp Coxeter tomography, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.22421827`;
3. planned Article III — stable Coxeter tomography: normalization, singular values, conditioning, oversampling, and robustness.

The paper intentionally separates proved mathematics from explicitly labelled conjectural physical/geometric targets.

## Post-audit mathematical spine

The publication build contains the following theorem-level statements as direct consequences of Article II:

- transport-restricted loop bound
  `||H_Phi-I|| <= (m-1) mu_Phi(S)`;
- exact flatness when the transport algebra lies in `MD(Phi)`;
- first-order dissipative invisibility on the common noise commutant;
- positivity
  `Gamma_L(A*,A)=sum_alpha [A,V_alpha]*[A,V_alpha] >= 0`
  for the displayed GKSL representation;
- fixed-loop small-time estimate
  `||H_t-I|| <= t(m-1) g_L(S)+O(t^2)` as `t -> 0`.

The next strict inverse/stability problem is

`L_d^rob(epsilon)=min{|D|: sigma_min(M_D)>=epsilon}`

after fixing a physically and mathematically meaningful normalization of `M_D`.

## Claim ceiling

The paper does **not** claim:

- spacetime curvature or a physical gauge field generated by context loss;
- equivalence with Uhlmann/Jamiolkowski channel holonomy;
- a universal decoherence or entropy-production measure;
- first practical Lindblad tomography;
- discovery of process tensors or non-Markovian process tomography;
- robustness, sample-complexity optimality, or operational optimality of the sharp Article-II Coxeter designs.

The long-range programme target remains explicitly interrogative:

`context loss -> stable loop observables -> environmental information -> effective geometry?`

## Literature boundary

The final build explicitly distinguishes the programme from established work on multiplicative domains, quantum error correction/noiseless structures, decoherence-free algebras, noncommutative carre du champ and quantum Markov geometry, channel holonomy, Lindbladian learning, and process tensors.

The four 2026 learning references `arXiv:2602.14694`, `arXiv:2606.18188`, `arXiv:2607.28610`, and `arXiv:2601.01806` remain labelled as preprints.
