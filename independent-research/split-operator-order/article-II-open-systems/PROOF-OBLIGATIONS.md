# Article II — proof obligations and claim firewall

## Proved in the current bounded seed

- UCP multiplicativity-defect recursion and loop bound.
- Multiplicative-domain flatness criterion.
- Stinespring defect formula.
- GKSL Leibniz-defect formula for bounded Lindblad operators.
- First-order reduced-loop expansion for uniformly continuous unital quantum dynamical semigroups.

## Not yet proved / not to be stated as theorem without completion

1. Universal monotonicity of reduced curvature under successive CP reductions.
2. Infinite-dimensional extension with unbounded GKSL generators.
3. Equality between reduced order curvature and any standard decoherence or information-loss monotone.
4. A general operational interpretation in terms of channel capacity, entropy production, or recoverability.
5. A universal relation to physical gauge curvature or spacetime curvature.
6. A theorem that every dissipative semigroup produces nonzero order curvature.

## Falsification criteria

The partial-context programme must be weakened or closed if:

- all physically natural channel reductions tested are multiplicative on the relevant transport algebra and yield trivial reduced holonomy;
- proposed curvature quantities depend primarily on arbitrary representation choices rather than the declared reduction;
- the first-order Lindblad defect has no stable extension beyond toy finite-dimensional examples;
- no invariant or operationally interpretable quantity survives changes of Stinespring dilation.
