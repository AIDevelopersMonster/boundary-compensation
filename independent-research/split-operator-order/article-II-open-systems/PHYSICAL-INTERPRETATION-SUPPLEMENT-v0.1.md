## Editorial decision

Do **not** rewrite or dilute the theorem paper v0.2.1 merely to make the physical intention more visible. Keep the rigorous article as the formal core and publish a separate companion supplement that states the physical direction explicitly, with a strict separation between:

1. theorem-level mathematics;
2. mathematically justified physical interpretation;
3. speculative/conjectural research targets.

The purpose is deliberate: if the physical target is never stated, readers may see only an operator-algebra/tomography construction and miss why the programme was built. Conversely, merging speculative claims into the proof paper would weaken the claim discipline that made Article II publishable.

## 1. Theorem-level core

The following remain established results of Article II:

- nested-reduction composition law;
- Stinespring leakage formula;

No physical ontology is required for these statements.

## 2. Physically motivated interpretation

The natural physical reading is not that reduced order holonomy is already a spacetime or gauge curvature. The supported interpretation is narrower:

> when controlled operator transport is exactly flat before reduction, a UCP reduction creates a loop defect precisely to the extent that the reduction fails to preserve products along the transport path.

In Stinespring form

`Delta_Phi(X,Y)=V^* pi(X)(I-VV^*) pi(Y)V`,

so the defect is generated by propagation through components outside the compressed subsystem image. This gives a concrete mathematical model of **context/information loss through environmental degrees of freedom**.

For a bounded GKSL generator,

`Gamma_L(X,Y)=sum_alpha [V_alpha^*,X][Y,V_alpha]`,

so the first-order loop defect measures incompatibility between the chosen transport operators and the environmental scattering/noise operators. In particular, if the relevant transport algebra commutes with all dissipative operators, the first-order defect disappears.

This suggests a physically meaningful diagnostic principle:

> loop-resolved multiplicativity defects can serve as structured probes of incompatibility between coherent control operations and dissipative couplings.

This is an interpretation of proved formulas, not a new experimental theorem.

## 3. Why the word “holonomy” is retained

The term is retained because the observable object is path/loop dependent and vanishes for the unreduced exactly flat transport. However, the term must always be qualified as

- `reduced order holonomy`,
- `reduced multiplicativity holonomy`, or
- `UCP-loop defect`,

and never presented without qualification as physical gauge/spacetime curvature.

The established channel holonomy literature based on Uhlmann/Jamiołkowski parallel transport is mathematically distinct.

## 4. Speculative physical programme

The following are explicit research targets, not claims of Article II.

### 4.1 Context-loss geometry

Investigate whether families of UCP reductions admit an effective connection/curvature formalism whose local curvature data are generated by `Delta_Phi` or `Gamma_L`. The goal is not to rename the existing defect, but to determine whether a genuine differential-geometric structure emerges under additional regularity and composition assumptions.

### 4.2 Environment-sensitive loop probes

Use controlled loop families to infer structure of the dissipative commutant and decoherence-free/noiseless sectors. The exact target is to characterize when

`Gamma_L(T_k,P_{k-1})=0`

for all faces of a design and relate that condition to protected subalgebras.

### 4.3 Distance to multiplicative behaviour

Develop quantitative measures of how far a reduction is from its multiplicative domain on a declared transport set, for example by norms of `Delta_Phi`, and relate these to loop amplification or cancellation.

### 4.4 Stable/operational tomography

Replace rank-only identifiability by singular-value and condition-number control, then translate stable Coxeter designs into experimentally implementable state-preparation/measurement protocols and sample-complexity bounds.

### 4.5 Non-Markovian extension

Investigate whether the nested-defect composition law has a natural process-tensor/memory-kernel analogue. This is not proved in Article II and requires a separate mathematical model.

### 4.6 Possible links to physical geometry

Only after a genuine connection/curvature structure is constructed should one ask whether reduced-order defects can model or approximate geometric phases, effective gauge curvature, or other physical geometric quantities. Any such link must be derived, not inferred from terminology.

## 5. Publication strategy

Recommended publication architecture:

- **Article II v0.2.1:** rigorous theorem paper; do not reopen except for genuine mathematical/citation defects.
- **Companion supplement / perspective note:** this document expanded into a citable note explaining physical motivation, interpretive scope, and conjectural targets.
- **Article III:** stable/robust Coxeter tomography: singular values, conditioning, oversampling, noise amplification.
- **Later physical paper:** operational protocol and/or non-Markovian extension, only after theorem-level results exist.

This preserves both scientific discipline and the original physical ambition of the programme.

## 6. Response to Review 2

Review 2 correctly stresses the special nature of the measurement model, the need for expanded proofs, the distinction from channel holonomy, the lack of conditioning/sample-complexity analysis, and the danger of overloading the terminology. It also recommends avoiding physical holonomy claims without an experimental model.

The programme accepts the technical cautions but does **not** adopt the stronger editorial conclusion that the physical direction should remain unstated. The physical objective will be stated explicitly in a separate interpretive layer with theorem/speculation labels.

The review's warning that CP reduction and the earlier purely combinatorial pair reduction are not the same operation is accepted and should be made explicit whenever the two papers are presented as a series.

## 7. Claim firewall for the supplement

Allowed language:

- `suggests`, `motivates`, `provides a candidate diagnostic`, `research target`, `conjectural extension`, `possible physical interpretation`.

Forbidden language unless separately proved:

- `is spacetime curvature`, `is gauge curvature`, `measures entropy production`, `is a universal decoherence measure`, `proves non-Markovianity`, `is experimentally optimal`, `is physically observed`.

## 8. Programme position

The split-interval/operator-order origin remains historically and conceptually relevant, but the mature mathematical apparatus is now C*-algebraic and open-system based. The project should therefore retain the physical ambition while requiring every new physical claim to pass through a formal mathematical interface.

**Decision:** keep Article II intact; publish a separate physical-interpretation/speculative supplement rather than suppressing the intended physics or weakening the theorem article.
