## 2. Symplectic-orbital skew maps

Let

\[

\[

\[

=\sum_{k\in G}\phi([k,h])W_k.

\tag{2.1}

Because `phi(0)=0`,

\[
D_\phi(I)=0.

### Lemma 2.1 — star preservation

`D_phi` is `*`-preserving.

#### Proof

Using `W_h^*=W_-h`,

\[
D_\phi(W_{-h})
=\sum_k\phi(-[k,h])W_k
=-\sum_k\phi([k,h])W_k.
\]

On the other hand,

\[
D_\phi(W_h)^*
=\sum_k\phi([k,h])W_{-k}
=-\sum_r\phi([r,h])W_r

after `r=-k`. QED.

### Lemma 2.2 — skew superoperator

In the Weyl operator Hilbert space,

\[
D_\phi^*=-D_\phi.
\]

#### Proof

The matrix kernel is

\[
K(k,h)=\phi([k,h]).
\]

Since

\[
K(h,k)=\phi([h,k])=-\phi([k,h]),
\]

the superoperator matrix is real skew-symmetric. QED.

The real odd-function space has dimension

\[
\boxed{

\tag{2.2}

Thus
